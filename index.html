Who is Kevin 认识凯文

From the world's stages
to the most intimate human territory 从世界顶级舞台
走入最深的人心领域

I began dancing as a child. By my twenties I stood on the world's most prestigious stages, trained by the greatest choreographers of my generation — learning to inhabit emotion so completely that an audience could feel it from a hundred rows away.

Then I discovered something: that ability didn't belong only on a stage. The same sensitivity that lets a dancer embody grief, joy, or fear — lets a healer feel what's happening in someone before a single word is spoken.

For the past 15 years I have brought that gift into healing work — combining body intelligence, family constellation therapy, somatic insight, and dynamic meditation into something that has no single name. It simply works.

I work with one issue per session. I don't keep clients for endless rounds. My track record with private clients is 100%. That's not a claim — it's a commitment to your time, and mine.

我从小学习舞蹈。二十多岁时，我站上了世界最顶级的舞台，向那个时代最伟大的编舞家学习——用身体诠释情感，让百米之外的观众都能感同身受。

后来我发现：这种能力不只属于舞台。让舞者能够具身呈现悲伤、喜悦或恐惧的那种敏锐——同样能让一位疗愈师，在来访者开口之前，就感知到他们内心正在发生什么。

过去15年，我将这份天赋带入疗愈领域——融合身体智慧、家族系统排列、躯体洞察与动态冥想，形成一种无法用单一名称概括的方法。它就是有效。

我每次只专注处理一个议题。我不相信让来访者无休止地回来。我对私人来访者的处理成功率是100%。这不是营销话术——这是我对你的时间和我自己时间的承诺。
